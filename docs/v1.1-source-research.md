# FormulaHub v1.1 source research

## Verified source families

- Physics: OpenStax *Physics*, Ch. 3 Key Equations — https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/3-key-equations
  - Covers average acceleration, average velocity, constant-acceleration velocity, displacement, and the velocity-position equation.
- Physics/electricity: OpenStax *University Physics Volume 2*, Ch. 5 Key Equations — https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/5-key-equations
  - Covers Coulomb's law and related electric-field equations.
- Chemistry: OpenStax *Chemistry 2e*, Ch. 1 Key Equations — https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-key-equations
  - Covers density and temperature conversion relationships.
- Statistics: OpenStax *Introductory Statistics 2e*, Appendix F — https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/f-mathematical-phrases-symbols-and-formulas
  - Covers factorial, combinations, binomial distribution, and statistical notation.

## Policy

Only sources whose exact formula or a directly equivalent relationship can be verified should be marked `verified: true`. Chapter/section fields remain omitted when an exact location is not confirmed. Additional mathematics, engineering, and finance sources will be researched before those formulas are marked as traceable.
